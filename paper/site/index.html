When a processor fails a test, a person opens a waveform viewer and scrolls. We joined the waveform, the commit log and the golden ISA trace on cycle, and asked whether an AI agent debugs better with the join than with the same bytes as text.

Try it · every killed mutant in the corpus

Why did the processor fail?

Pick a defect. WaveQL joins a waveform of up to 124 MB with the commit log and the Spike divergence, then walks back through the generated netlist from the failure, following only the operands that account for each value. This is exactly what the agent receives in its first tool call.

How we got here

Chasing the bottleneck up the stack

The first measurement said nothing, and the reason was the evidence rather than the agent. Each time we repaired what stood between the evidence and the answer, the bottleneck moved one layer up.
